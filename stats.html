  • Don't count your own visits: open elmerson.me/?notrack once on each of your phones and laptops. Undo with ?track.
  • See exactly where a link brought people from by tagging it: elmerson.me/?ref=whatsapp, ?ref=linkedin, ?ref=resume, ?ref=qr. WhatsApp and most apps hide the referrer, so untagged shares show up as “Direct”.
